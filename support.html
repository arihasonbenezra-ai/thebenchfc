Common questions

Do I need an account to use The Bench?
Yes — creating a free account lets your followed teams, predictions, and points sync to your account and carry over if you reinstall or switch devices.
How do I delete my account?
Open the app, go to Settings, and choose Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and data and can't be undone.
I found a bug or something looks wrong.
Please email support@senylabs.io with what you saw and, if you can, a screenshot — it helps us fix it faster.
Is The Bench affiliated with any club or league?
No. The Bench is an independent fan app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any football club, league, or federation.
